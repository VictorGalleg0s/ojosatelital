Ojo Satelital – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Summary

Ojo Satelital is a desktop tool used only by the owner of the Ojo Satelital TikTok account to upload his own videos as drafts. It does not collect, sell or share data about other people.

2. Data the app uses

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): the account's display name and avatar, only to show which account is connected.
  • Access token: issued by TikTok after login, used only to upload videos as drafts (scope video.upload).
  • The videos that the account owner chooses to upload.

3. Where data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. The access token is stored in a local file that is never shared or sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API. There are no external servers, analytics or advertising.

4. Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Data is only sent to TikTok, through its official API, to upload the drafts.

5. Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by the adult account owner.

7. Contact

Send a message to @ojosatelital on TikTok or YouTube.
