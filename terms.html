Ojo Satelital – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. What the app is

Ojo Satelital is a desktop tool used by the owner of the Ojo Satelital channel to upload videos he made himself (planet Earth seen from satellites) to his own TikTok account. Videos are sent to the TikTok inbox as drafts; the account owner reviews and publishes them manually inside the TikTok app.

2. Who can use it

The app is for personal use by the account owner only. It is not offered to the public and it does not have other users.

3. TikTok account connection

Connecting a TikTok account is done through TikTok's official Login Kit. You can disconnect the app at any time from your TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's Terms of Service.

4. Content

All uploaded videos are original works of the account owner, or use public-domain or openly licensed material credited in the description. Scenes generated with AI are labelled as such. The account owner is responsible for the content he publishes.
